# 🏠 Online Home Services

**Online Home Services** is a comprehensive on-demand platform designed to simplify household task management by seamlessly connecting users with skilled and verified service providers. Whether it’s plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, or carpentry, our platform ensures convenience, reliability, and efficiency at your fingertips.

## 📖 About the Project

Online Home Services aims to bridge the gap between customers and trusted professionals for a wide range of home-related services. With a user-friendly interface, robust authentication, fair pricing, and a focus on customer satisfaction, the platform enhances how people manage their home service needs in today’s fast-paced world.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Replace manual service booking with a streamlined digital process.
* Provide a **user-friendly** and intuitive interface for effortless navigation.
* Ensure **trusted and background-checked professionals**.
* Maintain **data privacy and user safety**.
* Offer a modern, efficient web experience for all users.

---

## 🔍 Scope

* A platform for booking services like:

  * Plumbing
  * Electrical work
  * Carpentry
  * Cleaning
  * Painting, etc.
* Serve homeowners, renters, and professionals.
* Enable service providers to connect with clients.
* Implement secure user authentication and encrypted data handling.
* Integrate payment gateway and feedback system.
* Use modern web frameworks and technologies.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 👤 User Module

* Sign up & Login
* View and update profile
* Book, cancel, or reschedule services
* Get notifications for service confirmation/rejection
* Download payment receipts
* Provide feedback

### 🧰 Service Provider Module

* Sign up & Login
* Manage personal details
* View/confirm/reject/cancel appointments
* Deactivate account

### 🛠️ Admin Module

* Admin login
* View/Update/Delete users and service providers
